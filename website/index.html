A CLEAR BOUNDARY

Your finance work
happens locally.

After setup, the app uses a service on your own computer. It has no cloud document-processing integration, bank connection, or automatic record uploads.

↳

This public website provides downloads and documentation. It has no place to enter financial records.

Does the app need internet?

Downloading the app and an optional model requires internet access. Once installed, ordinary finance work runs offline. Optional AI uses a separate local Ollama process and a model you have downloaded yourself.

What does “local” protect?

The app’s finance service stays on your computer. Your browser, operating system, other installed software, and cloud-synchronized folders have their own behavior and access. The app is not disk encryption or proof that your entire PC has no network traffic. Use an appropriate local storage location and device protections.

What happens to my data?

Approved records and imported originals are saved in your local workspace. Its exact location is shown in Settings. Exports and backups contain your information in plaintext. Unsent browser drafts are not persisted. Use the app’s confirmed shutdown control when you want to stop its local service.

Is AI required?

No. Manual entry, budgets, file previews, and exports work without AI. Optional AI requires a separate Ollama installation and compatible local model. Suggestions can be wrong; review every proposed entry. Models and Ollama are not bundled.

Is this ready for every situation?

This is an unsigned pre-release. Fictional-data testing does not guarantee a bug-free or universally secure application. Fresh-PC validation and an independent security audit are not complete. It is a personal recordkeeping tool, with no tax filing, payment, live bank feed, or multi-currency conversion.

Does this website track me?

We add no analytics scripts, remote fonts, advertising, accounts, or upload forms. GitHub hosts this page and logs visitors’ IP addresses for its security purposes. Download requests also go to GitHub. This website’s hosting is separate from the installed app’s local processing.
